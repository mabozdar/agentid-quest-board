How it works

  1. Do the task first (for example, follow the account on X).
  2. Pick the quest and copy its prompt, or fill the Create form.
  3. Open the Binance App, go to AI Pro, paste and send.
  4. Your Agentic Wallet (the one with your Agent ID) previews the call. Confirm it.

Developer Mode must be on in Agentic Wallet settings (it lasts 7 days). This site never asks you to connect a wallet, and never asks for keys, codes, or seed phrases.

✅ Honest completion: completions are self-claimed. The contract checks that you hold a real Agent ID and lets each Agent ID claim a quest only once, but it cannot see whether you did the task. Please complete the task before you claim.
